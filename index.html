Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete a website?

Once all content and photos are supplied, our typical turnaround time for a Basic Package is 2–3 working days. Standard multi-page sites are typically delivered within 4–7 working days.

Do you work with clients outside of Margate & Port Shepstone?

Yes! While we are based in Margate & Port Shepstone, we serve businesses nationwide across South Africa. Everything is managed smoothly online via WhatsApp, email, and phone calls.

Can we edit our own website content after it goes live?

Yes. Our Standard Package includes a dedicated client admin portal where you can edit text, services, and photos through a simple web form without needing coding knowledge.

What are your deposit and payment terms?

We require a 50% deposit before starting design work, with the remaining 50% balance due upon final sign-off before your site goes live on your permanent domain.

What is included in the R300/month maintenance add-on?

Our optional R300/mo package covers high-speed web hosting management, SSL security, domain renewal oversight, continuous performance checks, and small routine content edits whenever you need them.
